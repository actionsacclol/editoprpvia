# Marketing Krypt Editor — a creator's cheat sheet

Krypt Editor is a **free, offline, no-watermark CapCut alternative for Windows**.
That sentence is the whole pitch. Everything below is just ways to say it in 15
seconds.

## The 3 angles that actually sell it

1. **Price rage** — CapCut Pro costs money. This costs nothing. Forever.
2. **Privacy** — captions, TTS, and background removal run *on your PC*. No
   upload, no account, no cloud.
3. **No watermark, no export limit** — the two things people search for.

Pick one angle per video. Never three.

## Rules for the clip

- **First 1.5 seconds decide everything.** Open on the result or the price tag,
  never on "hey guys".
- **Screen recording > talking head.** Record the editor at 60fps, zoom into the
  cursor, keep it moving.
- **Under 20 seconds** for the hook-y ones, 30–45s for tutorials.
- **Text on screen from frame one**, big, top-third, readable with sound off.
- **End on the same CTA every time:** "krypt.cc — free, no sign-up."
- Post the same clip to TikTok, Reels, and Shorts. Don't reshoot for each.

## 12 reel ideas that travel

**Price / rage-bait (highest ceiling)**

1. Split screen: CapCut paywall popup on the left, the same feature working free
   on the right. No voiceover, just a timer counting how long the edit took.
2. "I cancelled my $89/year editor" → screen record the cancel page, then cut
   straight into exporting a 4K clip with no watermark.
3. "Things CapCut charges for that this doesn't" — rapid-fire list, one feature
   per second, 10 features, done.

**Satisfying / no-talking**

4. Green screen removal in one click. Raw clip → click → subject floating on a
   new background. Loop it so it replays seamlessly.
5. Auto-captions generating live, word by word, karaoke highlight on. This one
   hypnotizes people — let it play full length, no cuts.
6. Speed ramp on a jump or a car pass. Show the velocity curve being dragged,
   then the result. Music hit lands on the ramp.
7. Drag a `.cube` LUT in, drag the strength slider left to right, flat footage
   turns cinematic. 8 seconds, no words.

**Proof / flex**

8. "Full edit, start to export, in 60 seconds" — real-time screen record,
   sped up, timer in the corner.
9. Airplane mode test: turn off wi-fi on camera, then generate captions and a
   voice-over anyway. "It's all local."
10. Export a 10-minute video and show the file size + zero watermark next to a
    competitor's export.

**Teach (best for saves and follows)**

11. "3 shortcuts that make you 2x faster" — split, ripple delete, Ctrl+wheel
    zoom. One per beat.
12. "How to get the TikTok caption look for free" — style the text, add the
    box highlight, export. Show the settings panel clearly so people re-watch.

## Hooks you can copy

- "This is CapCut Pro. This is free. Same edit."
- "Free video editor, no watermark, no account, works offline."
- "I didn't upload a single file and it still captioned my video."
- "Delete CapCut."
- "Windows editor that does the paid stuff for $0."

## Caption + tags

Keep the caption one line and put the link in bio, not in the caption.

> Free, offline, no watermark. Windows only. Link in bio.

Tags — mix broad and intent-based, 5–8 max:
`#capcutalternative #freevideoeditor #videoediting #editingtips #nowatermark
#contentcreator #windows #kryptEditor`

## Where to post beyond shortform

- **r/VideoEditing, r/NewTubers, r/software** — post the tool, not an ad. Lead
  with "I got tired of paying for CapCut" and answer every comment.
- **YouTube Shorts descriptions** get indexed by Google. Put "free CapCut
  alternative for Windows" in every one.
- Reply to comments on *other people's* "best free editor?" videos with an
  honest recommendation. That converts better than your own posts.

## Don't

- Don't fake a testimonial or a download count.
- Don't say "AI-powered" — say "captions in one click".
- Don't over-edit the demo. Choppy 20-cut edits hide the product.
